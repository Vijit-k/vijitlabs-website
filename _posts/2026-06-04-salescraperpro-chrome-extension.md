If you're in B2B sales, you know this routine.

Open LinkedIn. Find a prospect. Copy their name. Switch to your CRM. Paste it. Go back to LinkedIn. Copy their job title. Switch tabs again. Paste. Repeat this 50 times a day, and you've burned anywhere from 45 minutes to an hour. On data entry. Not selling.

That is not a productivity problem. That is a tooling problem.

## What SalesScraper Pro Does

SalesScraper Pro is a Chrome extension that pulls prospect data directly from LinkedIn profiles and exports it in a clean, usable format, without you touching a spreadsheet manually.

You visit a LinkedIn profile. The extension reads the page. You get the data you actually need: name, job title, company, location, and more, ready to copy or export as a CSV.

No scraping scripts. No Python setup. No API keys. Install it from the Chrome Web Store, and it works.

## Who This Is For

**Sales reps and SDRs** who build their own lists and are tired of copying data between tabs. If your quota depends on volume, every minute of manual data entry is a minute not spent on conversations that close deals.

**Founders doing their own prospecting.** Especially in the early stage when there is no dedicated sales hire. You know who you want to reach. You just need a faster path from "found them on LinkedIn" to "in my outreach tool."

**Recruiters and growth operators** who research people on LinkedIn regularly and need to move data somewhere without building a workflow from scratch.

If you are already on LinkedIn Sales Navigator, SalesScraper Pro works alongside it. Free or Premium account works too.

## What Makes It Different

There are other scraping tools. A few are expensive. Several break after LinkedIn updates its page structure. Most were built for technical users.

SalesScraper Pro is built for people who just want the data, fast.

Three things that matter:

**It runs in your browser.** No server involved, no third-party storing your search data. The extension processes what you are viewing on your screen.

**The output is clean.** You do not get a raw dump of every field on the page. You get the fields that actually go into a CRM or outreach tool, formatted consistently.

**No learning curve.** Install it. Open a LinkedIn profile. Done. No dashboard to configure, no onboarding flow to sit through.

## The Real Problem It Solves

Manual prospecting at scale is where sales productivity quietly dies. Most reps do not notice how much time they are spending because it is spread across 50 small actions throughout the day.

The math is straightforward. If you spend 45 minutes on data entry daily across a 250-day sales year, that is over 187 hours. More than four full 40-hour weeks spent on work that does not require your expertise and does not move a single deal forward.

SalesScraper Pro does not close deals for you. But it gives back the time you are currently losing to work that a tool should handle.
